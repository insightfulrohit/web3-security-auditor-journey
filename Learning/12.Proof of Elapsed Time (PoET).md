# Proof of Elapsed Time (PoET)

Proof of Elapsed Time (PoET) is a consensus mechanism designed to ensure fair and efficient block creation in distributed networks.

Instead of relying on computational power (PoW) or stake (PoS), PoET uses a **trusted execution environment (TEE)** to randomly assign wait times to participants.

---

## How the Selection Works

In PoET, each participating node requests a random wait time from a secure hardware component (such as Intel SGX).

The node with the **shortest wait time** gets the right to create the next block.

Key factors:

- Random wait time generation
- Trusted hardware enforcement
- Fair and unbiased selection

---

## Block Creation Process

1. Each node requests a random wait time from the TEE
2. The TEE generates a secure, verifiable timer
3. Nodes wait for their assigned time
4. The node whose timer expires first creates the block
5. Other nodes verify the legitimacy of the wait time
6. If valid, the block is added to the blockchain

---

## Role of Trusted Execution Environment (TEE)

- Ensures randomness cannot be manipulated
- Provides cryptographic proof of wait time
- Prevents nodes from cheating or skipping wait periods

Example technology:
- Intel SGX (Software Guard Extensions)

---

## Rewards and Fairness

- The winning node (shortest wait time) receives the block reward
- No need for high computational resources
- Equal opportunity for all participants

---

## What About Other Nodes?

- Other nodes continue participating in the process
- They verify the winning node’s proof
- They compete again in the next round

---

## Why It is Called Proof of Elapsed Time

Because nodes must prove they have **waited for a randomly assigned amount of time**, this mechanism is called **Proof of Elapsed Time**.

---

## Key Differences from PoW and PoS

- No heavy computation (unlike PoW)
- No staking required (unlike PoS)
- Relies on trusted hardware
- Energy-efficient and scalable
- Faster consensus in permissioned networks

---

## Summary

Proof of Elapsed Time (PoET) is a consensus algorithm that uses secure hardware to assign random wait times to nodes. The node with the shortest wait time gets to create the next block, ensuring fairness, efficiency, and low energy consumption.
